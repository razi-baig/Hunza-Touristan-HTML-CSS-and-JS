Explore Our Destinations

From breathtaking mountain peaks to historic villages, discover some of the most beautiful places in Hunza.

Hon Pass

Located above Karimabad, Hon Pass offers spectacular panoramic views of Hunza Valley and the surrounding Karakoram mountains. It is an unforgettable destination for adventure seekers and mountain lovers.

Explore More

Altit Village

Experience the charm of Altit Village, where traditional architecture, peaceful streets, and stunning mountain landscapes create a truly unforgettable atmosphere.

Explore More

Lady Finger Peak

Lady Finger Peak is one of the most iconic mountain formations in Hunza. Its distinctive sharp rock spire rises dramatically above the valley, creating a breathtaking view for visitors and photographers.

Explore More

Ganish Village

Explore Ganish, one of the oldest settlements along the historic Silk Road. Discover its ancient watchtowers, traditional mosques, heritage houses, and rich cultural history.

Explore More

Aliabad

Aliabad is the commercial and administrative center of Hunza. Enjoy its local markets, restaurants, cultural atmosphere, and beautiful views of the surrounding mountains.
